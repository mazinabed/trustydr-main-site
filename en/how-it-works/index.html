How it works

TrustyDr is fully web-based — no app needed. Here's how it works for patients and doctors.

TrustyDr runs entirely in your browser. Patients book appointments online, and doctors manage everything through the Doctor Portal — no downloads required.

For Patients

  1. Open the patient portal
    Visit TrustyDr in your browser and create your free account.
  2. Find a doctor
    Search by city, specialty, and availability.
  3. Request an appointment
    Choose a suitable time and submit your request online.
  4. Manage your visits
    Track upcoming appointments and receive reminders from your browser.

For Doctors

  1. Sign in to the Doctor Portal
    Go to doctor.trustydr.com and log into your account.
  2. Complete your profile
    Add details to help patients choose confidently.
  3. Set your availability
    Define your working hours and appointment slots.
  4. Receive bookings
    Review and manage incoming appointment requests online.
