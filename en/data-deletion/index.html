Data Deletion Instructions

Data Deletion

Effective date: January 17, 2026

TrustyDr provides ways to delete your account and associated personal data across the TrustyDr ecosystem — for Patient accounts, Healthcare Provider accounts, and TrustyDr Commerce merchant/business accounts.

Request deletion by email

Email support@trustydr.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request”. Please tell us which TrustyDr platform your account is on (Patient, Healthcare Provider, or Commerce) and include the email or phone number used for your account.

Deletion timeline

We typically process deletion requests within 30 days.

What we retain

TrustyDr keeps certain records even after account deletion, where required or where deleting them would break records that other people or businesses legitimately rely on:

  • Completed appointment and order records may be retained as historical receipts.
  • Limited data may be retained for legal, security, tax/accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Where data must be retained, we restrict it and keep only what is necessary.
