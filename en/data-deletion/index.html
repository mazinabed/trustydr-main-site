Data Deletion Instructions

Data Deletion

Effective date: January 17, 2026

TrustyDr provides ways to delete your account and associated personal data.

Option 1: Delete your account in the TrustyDr app (recommended)

  1. Open the TrustyDr app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select Account
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm deletion

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Email support@trustydr.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request” and include the email or phone number used for your TrustyDr account.

Deletion timeline

We typically process deletion requests within 30 days. If we must retain limited data for legal or security reasons, we will restrict it and retain only what is necessary.
