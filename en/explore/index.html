  1. Step 1

    Your practice home

    Verification and subscription status at the top, with direct access to your schedule, appointments, patient QR code and clinic.

  2. Step 2

    Schedule and availability

    Build a weekly pattern of shifts that repeat. Only the times you activate are offered to patients.

  3. Step 3

    Reception board

    The live queue — waiting, in progress and completed — with walk-ins added alongside booked appointments.

  4. Step 4

    Appointments

    Today, the next seven days, or any single day. Each booking carries the patient, the doctor and its payment state.

  5. Step 5

    The visit record

    Clinical notes, visit photos, lab and imaging requests, and the prescription — written or attached from the same screen.

  6. Step 6

    Issue a prescription

    Write the prescription on the visit, then print it for the patient or send it straight to a pharmacy. It stays on the record either way.

  7. Step 7

    Medication library

    Your centre’s own list of medications, drawn from the TrustyDr catalogue, with favourites and reusable prescription sets beside it.

  8. Step 8

    Your patient QR code

    A QR code for your public profile. Put it on the reception desk and patients can view your profile and book.

  1. Step 1

    Your pharmacy home

    Verification and subscription status at the top, with opening hours, your QR code and your facility a tap away.

  2. Step 2

    Opening hours

    Set opening and closing times for each day, and close the days you do not trade. Patients see when you are open.

  3. Step 3

    Prescriptions arrive

    A prescription sent by a doctor lands in your reception board with the patient, the prescriber and the medication. Confirm receipt to take it on.

  4. Step 4

    Preparation board

    Received, in preparation, and ready — every prescription your team is working on, in one place.

  5. Step 5

    Dispensing, with or without Store inventory

    Dispense against TrustyDr Store inventory when you use it, or record what you handed over manually. A pharmacy with no Store inventory works exactly the same.

  6. Step 6

    Collect and hand over

    Ready prescriptions show what is owed. Take payment and dispense in one step, or dispense straight away if it is already paid.

  7. Step 7

    A verifiable prescription

    Every prescription prints with its reference and a QR code that confirms it against the record TrustyDr issued.
