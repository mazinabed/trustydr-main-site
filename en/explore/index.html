Doctor Portal

Clinic, appointments and prescriptions in one place.

  1. Step 1

    Your practice home

    Verification and subscription status at the top, with direct access to your schedule, appointments, patient QR code and clinic.

  2. Step 2

    Schedule and availability

    Build a weekly pattern of shifts that repeat. Only the times you activate are offered to patients.

  3. Step 3

    Reception board

    The live queue — waiting, in progress and completed — with walk-ins added alongside booked appointments.

  4. Step 4

    Medication library

    Your centre’s own list of medications, drawn from the TrustyDr catalogue, with favourites and reusable prescription sets beside it. Medication names are standardised and never translated.

  5. Step 5

    Practice insights

    Appointments, completed visits, no-shows, cancellations and revenue — for the day, the week or the month.

  6. Step 6

    Your patient QR code

    A QR code for your public profile. Put it on the reception desk and patients can view your profile and book an appointment.
