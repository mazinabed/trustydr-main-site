Data & Account Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your TrustyDr account and personal data. It is a help page, not a separate legal agreement — deletion and retention are governed by the Privacy Policy that applies to the TrustyDr service your account is on.

How to request deletion

Email us at support@trustydr.com. Please tell us which TrustyDr service your account is on — TrustyDr Healthcare (as a Patient or as a Healthcare Provider) or TrustyDr Commerce — and include the email address or phone number used for that account.

What we retain

Your account and related data are retained while your account is active.

Acceptance-history and audit-log records — the record of which document version you accepted, and when — are retained permanently for accountability purposes, regardless of account status.
