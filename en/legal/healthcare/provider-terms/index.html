Iraq currently has no dedicated telemedicine or digital-health law: general medical-licensing and liability rules apply by default to remote or virtual communication with a patient exactly as they would to an in-person encounter. You are solely responsible for deciding whether a given case is appropriate to handle remotely, for the accuracy of any advice, note, or prescription you give or record through the App, and for recognizing the limits of what can safely be assessed without an in-person examination.
