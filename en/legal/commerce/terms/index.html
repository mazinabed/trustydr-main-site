"Merchant" means a business that lists and sells products through the Platform.

4. What TrustyDr Commerce is — and is not

TrustyDr Commerce is a technology platform. We provide the marketplace infrastructure that lets Merchants list products and manage orders, and that lets you browse, order, and communicate with Merchants. We are not a party to the contract of sale between you and a Merchant. Each Order is a transaction between you and the Merchant; the Merchant is the seller of record.

5. TrustyDr Commerce and healthcare/pharmacy products

This section applies specifically where products offered through the Platform are medicines, health products, or other items subject to healthcare or pharmaceutical regulation.

TrustyDr Commerce is not a pharmacy. We do not dispense medicine, do not practice medicine or pharmacy, do not provide medical or clinical advice, and do not verify prescriptions. Any Merchant offering medicines or regulated health products through the Platform is independently responsible for holding and maintaining every license, registration, and approval required by Iraqi law to sell those products lawfully — including, where applicable, licensing under the Pharmacy Practice/Pharmaceutical Law and registration of the product itself with the Iraq Drug Regulatory Authority — and for complying with all restrictions on prescription-only medicines, controlled substances, and the advertising of medicines.

TrustyDr Commerce is a distinct service from TrustyDr Healthcare (our related platform for doctor appointments and patient care). The two services are operated separately, and using one does not make you a user of the other.

6. Orders, pricing, and how a contract is formed

Product prices displayed on the Platform are set by the Merchant and are shown inclusive of applicable fees and taxes known at the time of listing. Before you submit an Order, you will be shown a summary of the transaction — including the Merchant's identity, the products ordered, the price, and delivery information — and you must affirmatively confirm the Order after reviewing that summary.

Submitting and confirming an Order is a request to purchase, sent to the Merchant — it is not yet a finalized sale. The Merchant may accept or reject your Order. A contract for the sale of the ordered products is formed between you and the Merchant only once the Merchant accepts your Order, subject to applicable law. If the Merchant rejects your Order, no sale occurs and no charge is finalized.

You may cancel an Order yourself, using the tools provided on the Platform, at any point before the Merchant has completed fulfilling it.

6A. How you pay for an Order

Payment for an Order is made directly between you and the Merchant — TrustyDr Commerce is not involved in, and does not process, hold, settle, or transfer, the funds for your purchase. Depending on what a Merchant offers, this may include paying in cash at the Merchant's location, paying cash on delivery, paying by card or another method directly with the Merchant, or paying through an electronic method the Merchant supports (for example, ZainCash or a similar service). The Platform may display or record the payment method used for an Order as part of its order-tracking tools, but TrustyDr Commerce is not a payment processor, acquiring bank, escrow provider, wallet provider, or settlement provider for these transactions, and does not hold or take custody of your purchase funds at any point.

This is separate from any subscription or platform fee a Merchant may pay to TrustyDr to use the Platform, which is not part of your relationship with TrustyDr as a customer. If TrustyDr introduces its own integrated payment processing in the future, that will be a distinct change requiring an updated version of these Terms.

7. Delivery

Delivery is arranged and performed by the Merchant, using the Merchant's own delivery personnel or arrangements. TrustyDr Commerce provides tools that let Merchants coordinate and track delivery status, but we do not operate a delivery fleet and are not the party responsible for the physical delivery of your Order.
