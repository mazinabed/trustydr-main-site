FAQ

Common questions about TrustyDr.

Is TrustyDr a medical provider?

No. TrustyDr is a platform that helps users connect with healthcare providers. Providers are responsible for medical care.

How do I access TrustyDr as a patient?

TrustyDr is a web-based platform — no download required. Simply open app.trustydr.com in your browser, create a free account, and start booking appointments.

Do I need to download anything?

No. TrustyDr runs entirely in your browser on any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile. You can also install it as a Progressive Web App (PWA) from your browser's menu for a faster experience.

How do doctors use TrustyDr?

Doctors use the Doctor Portal at doctor.trustydr.com to manage their profile, set availability, and handle appointment requests.

What languages is TrustyDr available in?

TrustyDr is available in English, Arabic, and Kurdish. You can switch languages at any time from the top navigation bar.

Can I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

Yes. You can manage, cancel, or reschedule appointments from your account on the patient portal.

How do I get support?

Email us at support@trustydr.com and we'll help you as soon as possible.

How can I delete my data?

Visit the Data Deletion page for steps to delete your account and associated data.
